\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
% W--Z: proposal, strengths to test, expected deliverables, future work.
This proposal sketches a running coach that can turn a short conversation into a plan and help the runner record the session. Weather and route tools would supply local context, while the scope check and daily quota would control model use. Speech and lip sync are intended to support hands-free feedback, and their responsiveness remains to be tested.

The first milestone is a public demo that can complete the planning and logging flow with a checked route and a manually entered distance. We also expect to provide the repository, design notes, and a demo recording. The evaluation will test whether the workflow fits the available cloud quotas. Continuous GPS, step and posture sensing, lower-cost avatar rendering, and longer user studies are possible follow-up work.
