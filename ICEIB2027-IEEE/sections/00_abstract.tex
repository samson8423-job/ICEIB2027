\begin{abstract}
About 1.8 billion adults worldwide do not meet recommended activity levels, and in a scoping review of lifestyle and mental-health apps, a median of 70\% of users discontinued use within 100 days. This paper presents AI Fitness Coach, a conversation-first domain AI agent for personalized running guidance. The system couples a single FitnessCoachAgent with a non-LLM ScopeGuard that filters intent and enforces a per-day token budget. Weather, route, activity logging, food-image estimation, and MCP-based nutrition suggestions are integrated behind a backend validation layer, and responses are rendered as text, cards, speech, and a lip-synced digital human. The prototype is built with Vanilla JS and FastAPI and deployed on Vercel with Supabase. We describe the design and a planned evaluation that combines human rating with automated metrics over end-to-end flows. Measured results are not yet available; the performance figures reported here are design targets, not experimental findings.
\end{abstract}
